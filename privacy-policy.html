Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including your name, phone number, email address, and business information when you submit a form on our website or contact us directly.

We use the information we collect to contact you about our services, respond to your inquiries, and send you SMS messages if you have opted in to receive them. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

By opting in to receive SMS messages from Nexora, you consent to receive marketing and informational text messages from Nexora at the phone number you provide. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for assistance.

No Sharing of SMS Opt-In Data: We do not sell, share, rent, lease, or otherwise disclose your SMS opt-in data, phone number, or any information related to your SMS consent to any third party or affiliate for marketing purposes. Your consent to receive SMS messages is collected solely for use by Nexora and will never be transferred to outside organizations.

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your browsing experience and analyze site traffic. Cookies are small files stored on your device. You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. If you do not accept cookies, some portions of our website may not function properly.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure. All data transmissions are encrypted using industry-standard SSL/TLS protocols. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of the personal information we hold about you. To opt out of SMS communications, reply STOP to any message we send. To request access to, update, or delete your information, contact us at rafaguer07@gmail.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at rafaguer07@gmail.com.
